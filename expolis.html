Science Notes

Colony model. Solar output uses Mars’s mean solar irradiance of about 586 W/m², scaled by daylight, panel efficiency, dust accumulation and storms. Battery energy is finite; demand exceeding generation plus usable storage causes prioritised load shedding. [1]

Atmosphere and life support. Mars’s atmosphere is predominantly CO₂ and contains only about 0.16% O₂, so oxygen must be generated, stored and recycled rather than extracted directly from ambient air. [1][2]

Orbital mechanics. For circular coplanar orbits, the Hohmann transfer semi-major axis is \(a_t=(r_1+r_2)/2\), transfer time is half the transfer-orbit period, and propellant is estimated with the Tsiolkovsky equation, \( \Delta v = I_{sp}g_0\ln(m_0/m_f) \). This is an educational approximation, not a validated mission-design tool. [11][13]

Navigation. Star observations primarily estimate spacecraft attitude/heading. Absolute position requires additional information such as ephemerides, range, Doppler, or known landmarks; the game models this distinction explicitly.

Units. Power: kW. Battery energy: kWh. Water: m³. Oxygen and food: supply units. Materials/parts: tonnes-equivalent. Funds: M₡. Time: Mars sols unless labelled Earth days.
