🚀 NOVARIS • FIELD GUIDE

Goal: Open the hatch, recover five floating items, repair the three station systems and return to the airlock. Follow the ACTIVE OBJECTIVE card.

Mobile controls

  • Left joystick: glide forward/backward and sideways.
  • Right joystick: aim and turn your view.
  • UP / DOWN: fly vertically; BRAKE: stop drifting.
  • INTERACT: open hatch or pick up nearby equipment.
  • SCAN: find the nearest objective. USE / REPAIR: fix a nearby terminal.
  • CAMERA: switch views; BOOST: move faster.

Keyboard: WASD move, mouse drag to look, R/F up/down, E interact, Q repair, T scan, Space brake, Shift boost.

Tip: Release the movement stick early and press BRAKE before interacting. Approach slowly and watch the Nearby indicator.
